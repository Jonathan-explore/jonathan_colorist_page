I come from content creation. Before I picked up the FX6, I grew a niche account from 300 to 3,000 followers organically and turned that attention into real income — so I think about the goal of a video, not just how it looks.

Now based in Randers, I shoot and edit my own work: camera, lighting, sound and color grading in DaVinci Resolve. My portfolio is young and honest — personal and spec projects for now — and it grows with every real shoot. I'm learning Danish and open to collaborating across Jutland.
